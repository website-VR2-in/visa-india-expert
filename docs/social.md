# Step 6 — Social Media Plan (visaindiaexpert)

## 1. Target audience (3 sentences)

1. Foreign nationals — investors, business people and employees — who must obtain or renew an Indian visa to run, fund or work on their business or role in India.
2. Their families (spouses, dependent children) who need parallel dependent visas at the same time.
3. First-time applicants who are unsure which visa category fits them and want to avoid a costly rejection.

## 2. Channels (2)

**LinkedIn (primary)** — the audience (investors, founders, HR/employment teams) lives here. Company page + founder profile.
**X / Twitter (secondary)** — fast tips, Q&A, keyword presence; supports the blog with shareable threads.

## 3. Handles (register everywhere)

- `visaindiaexpert` — LinkedIn company page, X handle, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- Same handle on: Telegram (channel for newsletter/newsletter-style updates), Threads
- If `visaindiaexpert` is taken anywhere, fall back to `visaindiaexpert_` (never a different word).

## 4. Three content pillars (90 days)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Practical visa knowledge** | 50% | "7 reasons India visas get rejected", processing-time threads, E-1 vs B-1 explained, document checklists, photo/passport rules |
| **B. Proof & process** | 30% | Case notes (anonymised): "Client's E-1 was refused because…", how the kickoff+success fee works, what a consultant checks before filing, WhatsApp support in action |
| **C. Authority & company** | 20% | Founder story (the 8-month E-1 lesson), pricing transparency ("you'll never be quoted privately"), press/About links, "ask a consultant" AMA posts |

## 5. Cadence

| Channel | Frequency | Times (CET) |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3/week | Tue 8am, Thu 8am, Fri 8am |
| X | 5/week | 8am, noon, 5pm (Mon–Fri) |
| Blog | 1/week | Friday (published before the weekly LinkedIn post) |

## 6. Per-platform formats (used by the auto-post agent)

- **LinkedIn** (~150 words): hook line → 3–5 short value paragraphs → end with a question. No hashtag spam; 2–3 max.
- **X** (3-tweet thread): tweet 1 = the hook/claim; tweets 2–3 = the substance; last line = CTA to the website page.
- **Blog intro** (250 words): SEO title + fact-dense intro that the full post expands.

## 7. Open Graph (already in code)

Every page sends `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image` (1200×630 brand image at `/og-image.png`) and `twitter:card=summary_large_image`. Test any URL at https://www.opengraph.xyz before posting.

| Metric | Where |
|---|---|
| Followers, impressions, clicks | LinkedIn analytics / X analytics |
| Site clicks from social (utm: `?src=li`, `?src=x`) | GA4 → Acquisition → Sessions by source/medium |
| Top posts (save as evergreen) | Spreadsheet tab "Content log" |
