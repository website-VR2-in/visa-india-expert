## How to use this calendar

**Friday publish workflow**

1. **Wednesday — post drafted.** Draft lands in `src/data/blog.ts` (via the
   auto-post pipeline) with title (≤ 60 chars, contains primary KW), meta
   description (150–160), JSON-LD dates, and the internal links listed above.
2. **Thursday — review.** Fact-check fees/processing times against the
   official portals; verify every internal link resolves; run Lighthouse
   (target: Perf/A11y/SEO/BP ≥ 90) and confirm the 3 social variants are
   approved in the review sheet (Score ≥ 7).
3. **Friday — publish before the LinkedIn post.** Post is live on the site
   before the 08:00 CET LinkedIn post that references it (i.e. by ~07:30 CET).
   Week 12 is the exception: publish Thu 2026-12-24 by ~07:30 CET, ahead of
   the Thursday post (12-25 is a holiday).
4. **At publish — update sitemap + internal links.** Add the new
   `/blog/<slug>` URL to `public/sitemap.xml` (20 URLs at launch; +1 per
   post), resubmit the sitemap in GSC, and add the post row to the tracking
   spreadsheet in [seo-keywords.md](seo-keywords.md).
5. **Log status** in the overview table: drafted → scheduled → published.

**Rules of thumb**

- If a publish slips, shift only that week — no back-to-back posts; the next
  post keeps its own Friday.
- Titles must contain the primary keyword; keep the slug, title and tracking
  sheet in sync.
- Every post links 2–3 internal URLs (one /visa/* hub, one related /blog/*
  post, /apply for the CTA) — the links above are the target set; adjust only
  if a referenced post is unpublished.
- The LinkedIn/X lines above are the brief for the auto-post agent's 3
  variants; the final copy lives in the review sheet.
